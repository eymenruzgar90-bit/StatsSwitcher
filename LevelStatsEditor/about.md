# Level Stats Editor

Right-click the **ⓘ (info) button** on a level to edit the **Attempts** and **Jumps** numbers that its info popup displays.

- **Done** saves your numbers for that one level.
- **Reset** (right under Done) removes your edit and shows the level's real numbers again.
- Left-clicking ⓘ works exactly like vanilla Geometry Dash.

## Important

This mod only changes what the info popup *displays*. Your real game statistics are never modified,
so the numbers you type are cosmetic. Every level is stored separately.
